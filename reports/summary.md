## Recommended actions

1. **Cut discounting by 4 points with a joint Marketing-Finance target.** Discount rate carries the largest negative weight in the margin model. Judge Marketing on gross margin per unit, not unit growth alone. *Impact: Owner: CMO + CFO*
2. **Clear 30% of excess inventory and tie purchase orders to demand.** Inventory leads discounts by a few months. Add a sell-through check before new orders. *Impact: Owner: COO*
3. **Slow headcount growth to 2% a year.** Labour grew faster than units. Hold back-office hiring until revenue per employee recovers. *Impact: Owner: CHRO*
4. **Replace team scorecards with one shared profit tree.** Every team hit its own number while profit fell. Review the cause links monthly as one leadership team. *Impact: +56.2M/qtr*
